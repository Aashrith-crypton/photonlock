# Cortex Clash SIH Template Notes

The shared **SIH 2026 Idea Presentation Format** sets a maximum of six slides including the title slide. Its fixed story is: Title Page, Idea Title, Technical Approach, Feasibility and Viability, Impact and Benefits, and Research and References.

The visual language is a light, spacious SIH-branded template: white and warm-light-gray background, blue curved footer band, compact SIH/AI emblem at the upper right, and a restrained technical wordmark/footer treatment. The adapted deck will retain this structure while adding a cozy scientific palette through muted navy, teal, warm amber, and soft cream optical-beam illustrations.
